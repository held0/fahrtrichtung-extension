# Privacy Policy – Fahrtrichtung Extension

Last updated: February 19, 2026

## What data is collected?

This extension does **not collect any personal data**. No accounts are created, no cookies are set, and no data is sent to our own servers.

## What data is sent to third parties?

To determine the travel direction, the extension makes the following requests:

1. **fernbahn.de** – Train type and train number are sent to retrieve the wagon order.
2. **bahn.expert** – Train type, train number, and travel date are sent to retrieve the station list and times.

These requests do not contain any personal data (no names, email addresses, or location data).

## What data is stored locally?

The extension stores only a usage counter (number of opens) in `chrome.storage.local`. This counter is used to occasionally show a donation prompt. No travel data, train information, or personal data is stored.

## Website access

The extension reads information from the open bahn.de page (train name, stations, travel date) to identify the correct train. This data is only processed locally and is not stored or forwarded.
