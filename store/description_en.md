# Store Listing: English

## Short Description (max 132 chars)
Shows the travel direction of long-distance trains during seat booking on bahn.de

## Full Description

Want to book a window seat facing forward – but bahn.de doesn't show which direction the train is going? "Train Direction" shows you the travel direction based on official Deutsche Bahn wagon order data. In rare cases, the data may change up until the day of travel – but it's better than no info at all!

How it works:
• Open bahn.de and search for a connection
• Click "Sitzplatz auswählen" (select seat)
• Click the extension icon in your browser toolbar
• The extension instantly shows the travel direction with an arrow

Features:
• Travel direction per route segment (including direction changes)
• Shows which wagon is at the front
• Travel time per segment
• Works with long-distance trains (ICE, IC/EC) as well as many international and night trains
• Automatic train detection from the seat selection dialog
• German and English

Data sources:
• Wagon order: fernbahn.de
• Station list and times: bahn.de

Open Source: https://github.com/held0/fahrtrichtung-extension
