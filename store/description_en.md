# Store Listing: English

## Short Description (max 132 chars)
Shows the travel direction of ICE/IC/EC trains on bahn.de – never sit backwards on the train again!

## Full Description

Ever booked a window seat facing forward – only to end up sitting backwards? With "Fahrtrichtung" you can see the travel direction of your train before booking your seat on bahn.de.

How it works:
• Open bahn.de and search for a connection
• Click "Sitzplatz auswählen" (select seat)
• Click the Fahrtrichtung icon in your browser toolbar
• The extension instantly shows the travel direction with an arrow

Features:
• Travel direction per route segment (including direction changes)
• Shows which wagon is at the front
• Travel time per segment
• Supports ICE, IC, EC, TGV, RJ and EST trains
• Automatic train detection from the seat selection dialog
• German and English

Data sources:
• Wagon order: fernbahn.de
• Station list and times: bahn.expert

Note: Wagon orders may change on the day of travel.

Open Source: https://github.com/held0/fahrtrichtung-extension
